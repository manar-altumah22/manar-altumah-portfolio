In development · My own product · Mobile app

Bridge

LinkedIn tells you who's connected. Bridge tells you who actually knows them. It's a mobile app for remembering the people you meet in real life and reaching new people through friends who know them.

  • Capture in 10 seconds: scan a LinkedIn QR code and record a voice note in English or Arabic. AI turns it into a contact card.
  • Ask your network: "Who can I talk to about data engineering in Toronto?" Bridge finds the best path, such as You → Maryam → Ahmed.
  • Warm intros with consent: your friend chooses whether to make the introduction.
  • Privacy by design: Postgres Row Level Security, opt-in sharing, and AI keys kept on the server.
